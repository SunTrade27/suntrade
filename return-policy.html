Return Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If you are not completely satisfied, you may return eligible items within 14 days of receiving your order.

1. Eligibility

You may return most items within 14 days of receiving your order. To qualify for a return, all of the following conditions must be met.

2. Conditions

To be eligible for a return:

  • Items must be unused and in original packaging
  • Proof of purchase (order number) is required
  • Customized or used products are not eligible

3. How to Return

Returning an item is simple:

  1. Contact us at support@suntrade.store with your order number
  2. We will provide the return instructions and address
  3. Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund will be issued within 5–10 business days

4. Defective Items

If your product arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 48 hours with photos. We will replace the item or refund you at no extra cost.

5. Return Shipping

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer. If the item is defective or we sent the wrong product, we cover the return shipping and provide a prepaid label when possible.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about returns or need help with an order, please contact us:

  • Email: support@suntrade.store
  • WhatsApp: +7 702 137 9248
