1. Shipping Coverage

We ship to most countries worldwide. Shipping costs and estimated delivery times are calculated and displayed at checkout before you complete your order.

2. Estimated Delivery Times

Delivery times are estimates and may vary due to customs processing, carrier delays, or other factors beyond our control. Typical delivery windows:

  • Central Asia & CIS: 7–15 business days
  • Europe: 10–20 business days
  • North America: 10–20 business days
  • Other regions: 15–30 business days

3. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs vary depending on the destination country, package weight, and selected shipping method. All applicable shipping costs are displayed at checkout before you confirm your order. We do not charge hidden fees.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can also track your order status at any time through your account page.

5. Customs & Import Duties

International orders may be subject to import duties and taxes imposed by the destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the buyer and are not included in the product price or shipping cost. We recommend checking with your local customs office for more information.

6. Shipping Address

Please ensure your shipping address is complete and accurate when placing your order. SunTrade is not responsible for orders shipped to incorrect or incomplete addresses provided by the customer. If you need to change your shipping address, contact us before your order is dispatched.

7. Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package is lost or arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours of the expected delivery date with photos of any damage. We will work with the carrier to investigate and either reship your order or issue a full refund.
